Schedule Sync

A personal automation that copies the account owner's entries from a published schedule PDF into a dedicated Google Calendar.

With the owner's authorization, the application creates and manages its own secondary calendar. Browser-based scheduled checks update existing entries when the source schedule changes.

The calendar is a convenience copy. Always consult the original published schedule for authoritative information.

This application is for its owner's personal use. It is not affiliated with Google or the publisher of the source schedule.
